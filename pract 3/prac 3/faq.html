Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about using StudentHub.

How do I create a StudentHub account?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, choose your course, and submit the form to create your account.

How can I register for classes?

After creating an account, log in and use the registration option on your dashboard to select your classes.

Where can I check my attendance and marks?

Log in to StudentHub and visit the Dashboard page to view your attendance and academic results.

How do I find upcoming college events?

Visit the Events page to see the latest college fests, seminars, workshops, and other activities.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check that you entered your registered email and password correctly. If the issue continues, contact the college administration.

How can I send feedback or ask for help?

Use the Feedback or Contact Us page to share your suggestion or send a question to the StudentHub team.

Need More Help?

If your question is not listed here, please contact the college administration through the Contact Us page.
