About StudentHub

StudentHub was created to help students and staff manage college activities online, like registration, attendance, and events, in one simple website.

Our Mission

Simplify Student Life

We want to make it easy for students to find information without standing in long queues.

Support Teachers Too

Teachers can also use the Admin page to manage student records and events.

Fun Fact
